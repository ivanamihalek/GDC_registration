# Obtaining the institutional eRA Commons account

Our ultimate goal is to gain access to a database (only one) called the "Genomic Data Commons Data Portal," 
which is located here: https://portal.gdc.cancer.gov/. 
More precisely, we would like to get the  access to the _controlled_ part of that database. 
There is also an open part, but from it we cannot see what is happening at the individual patient level.

The process is somewhat complicated; the diagram is below, but it starts with an eRA Commons account.
Scientists cannot create their own eRA account; their institution must do it for them. (See 
[here](https://www.era.nih.gov/register-accounts/create-and-edit-an-account.htm#find:~:text=Only%20officials%20from%20your%20own%20organization%20can%20create%20a%20new%20eRA%20Commons%20account) ), through their eRA Commons account.

Here are the step-by-step instructions for opening an Institutional eRA Commons account.
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CB5I-k_oB9s

However, opening an institutional eRA Commons account also has a pre-requirement, 
which is that the Institution (the Faculty of Medicine in our case) has a Unique Entity Identification Number, UEI (Unique Entity Identifier), very similar to our OIB. 
The reason is that the U.S. government conducts an independent verification of the existence and uniqueness of the institution before assigning that number.

Here are the step-by-step instructions for obtaining an institutional UEI:
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4RSHjczdxq8


After the Faculty receives the UEI, and then the institutional eRA Commons account, the responsible person will create an account for the PI.
